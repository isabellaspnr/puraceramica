Open Studio Clayground

We're so excited to launch our membership programme soon! We want to offer everyone who loves working with clay a space to create independently while being part of a creative community.

Join us soon for some Clay Time. Whether you're developing an ongoing project, practising a specific technique, or simply want a few uninterrupted hours at the wheel — you'll find it here. Use the space as your own: pottery wheels, handbuilding tools, clay and kiln firings included, plus a few extra surprises…

Who is it for?

Anyone who already has some experience working with clay and wants to refine their skills during independent studio time.
