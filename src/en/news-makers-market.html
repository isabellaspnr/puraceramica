Makers Market.

The year is coming to an end, and we're really happy to close it with something special: our very first Makers Market!

The studio has grown this year, and we're especially grateful to the many talented ceramic artists who have taught workshops here and shared their knowledge with all of us. Whether on the wheel, in handbuilding or in one of our more unusual workshops like Kintsugi, we've learned a lot, been amazed and, of course, had a great time.

At the Makers Market you can meet many of these talented artists, talk to them, see their work and take it home with you. A sure way to make someone very happy this Christmas (or yourself). We're always looking for artists who love being creative and want to show their work, and we hope to create a space where creative minds can come together.

Just like at our Summer Party, we'll also be selling ceramics that were painted here but never collected. All proceeds go to a social project or an animal shelter.

And for a festive atmosphere, we'll have tea, alcohol-free punch and Christmas cookies waiting for you, while stocks last.

When and where

Sunday, 13 December 10:00 – 19:00 Free entry Rua Jau 23a, Alcântara
