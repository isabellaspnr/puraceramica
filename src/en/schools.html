A different class for your students

We welcome your class at our studio in Alcântara. Students choose a ceramic piece, paint it, and take it home once fired. For school groups, it's also possible to agree on the same piece for everyone at the same price — for that, we ask that you get in touch by email before booking.

Up to 30 students · 2–3h · Alcântara, Lisbon
