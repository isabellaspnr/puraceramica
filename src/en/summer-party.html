Summer Party.

This year our summer party is a charity ceramics fair. A whole day of clay, music, cake and drinks, with the proceeds from the fair going to a local animal shelter. Come as you are, stay as long as you like. Entry is free.

Saturday, 1 August 12:00 – 20:00 Free entry Rua Jau 23a, Alcântara

What's happening

  • Open studio Pottery wheel and painting, all day long.
  • Charity fair Ceramics in aid of a local animal shelter.
  • Cake, drinks and music In partnership with @oxecoffee_, just next door.
  • Paint a tile Free. Give ceramic painting a go, no strings attached.
  • Studio tours See the space, the kilns and meet the team.
  • Raffle Studio vouchers and prizes.

Wheel Tryout: try the wheel, for free

Ever wondered how it feels to make something on the pottery wheel? On this day you can find out. Thirty-minute sessions where you try to make your own piece, with a teacher explaining the basics. It's not a workshop, it's an experience: what counts is the feeling of clay spinning between your hands.

Wheel sessions sold out - come by anyway!

The vote

At the end of the day we vote on the three best pieces: most creative, most beautiful and best disaster. Nobody makes anything decent on their first try, and that's half the fun. Our visitors vote, and the three winners get a voucher for one of our workshops.

Good to know

  • No experience needed. The teacher centres the clay for you, so you start at the fun part.
  • Sessions start at 13:00, 15:00 and 17:00. Please arrive five minutes early.
  • Wear something you don't mind getting dirty. We have aprons, but clay travels.
  • The session itself is free. If you want to keep your piece, we fire it for €5 and you can collect it at the studio about three weeks later. Otherwise the clay goes back in the bin and gets reused.
  • The three winning pieces are fired on us.
