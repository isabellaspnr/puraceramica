Wine & Clay

Slow down, get your hands in clay and enjoy a relaxed Friday evening in the studio. Wine & Clay combines free handbuilding, instructor support and a social atmosphere.
This event is hosted in Portuguese and English.

About this workshop

Wine & Clay is a relaxed social pottery session rather than a structured pottery class. You'll have time to explore handbuilding freely, work on your own idea and enjoy the evening with other makers.

Your instructor is there to help with techniques, construction and creative ideas whenever you need support.

No previous pottery experience is required. Come on your own, with a friend or as a small group.

What's included

Everything you need for a relaxed creative evening is provided in the studio.

01

Clay & materials

Clay and the basic materials needed for your handbuilding project.

02

Studio tools

Use of our handbuilding tools and equipment throughout the session.

03

Instructor guidance

Amanda will be available throughout the evening to help with techniques, construction and creative ideas.

04

Wine

Wine is included as part of the evening. A non-alcoholic option is also available.

Before you come

A few practical things to know before joining Wine & Clay.

18+ only

Wine & Clay is an adults-only event for participants aged 18 and over.

No experience needed

You don't need any previous pottery experience. The session is beginner friendly.

What to wear

Clay can get messy, so we recommend comfortable clothes that you don't mind getting a little dirty.

Come your way

You're welcome to come on your own, with a friend or as a small group.
