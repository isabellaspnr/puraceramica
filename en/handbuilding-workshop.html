About this workshop

Handbuilding gives you the freedom to create with clay without using a pottery wheel. In this workshop, you'll work at your own pace and create a ceramic piece of your choice with guidance from your instructor.

You'll be introduced to fundamental handbuilding techniques and learn which approach works best for your idea. Whether you'd like to make a cup, bowl, small vase, candle holder or something completely different, your instructor will support you throughout the process.

No previous pottery experience is required. Returning makers are equally welcome to use the session to develop their own projects.

What you can make

Create something that feels like your own. You can come with an idea or decide together with your instructor once you arrive.

01

Cup or Mug

Create a functional cup, mug or handle-less vessel in your own shape and style.

02

Bowl

Build a small serving bowl, breakfast bowl or an organic sculptural form.

03

Small Vase

Explore height, shape and texture while creating a small vase or vessel.

04

Candle Holder

Design a decorative object such as a candle holder, incense holder or similar piece.

Projects should be achievable within a single 2.5-hour session. Very large or technically complex pieces may require additional time.

Techniques you can explore

Your instructor will help you choose the technique that best suits the piece you want to create. You don't need to use every technique in one session.

01

Pinching

Shape clay directly with your hands to create cups, bowls and organic forms.

02

Coiling

Build forms gradually by stacking and joining coils of clay. A versatile technique for both functional and sculptural pieces.

03

Slab building

Work with rolled sheets of clay that can be cut, folded and joined to create structured forms.

04

Surface decoration

Add texture, patterns and painted details to give your piece its own character.

What's included

Everything you need to create your piece is provided in the studio.

01

Clay & materials

All clay and basic materials needed during the session.

02

Studio tools

Full use of our handbuilding tools and equipment during the workshop.

03

Instructor guidance

Individual support with your idea, technique and construction throughout the session.

04

Glazing & firing

Finishing and firing of your ceramic piece after the workshop.

Before you come

A few practical things to know before your workshop.

What to wear

Clay can get messy, so we recommend comfortable clothes that you don't mind getting a little dirty.

What to bring

You don't need to bring any materials or tools. If you already have an idea for your piece, you're welcome to bring reference images.

Finished pieces

Your piece will stay with us after the workshop for drying, glazing and firing. It will usually be ready for collection within 3–4 weeks.

Age

This workshop is suitable for participants aged 14 and over.
